## 5-minute live demo

### 0:00 – The problem (30 s, no screen yet)

> "Aminata grows rice in Bombali. At harvest she's paid in cash, spends it within weeks, and at planting time she has nothing left for seed or fertiliser. She has no records, so no bank will lend to her. Sierra Leone has 2.5 million mobile money wallets, but Aminata's harvest money still doesn't help her next season."

### 0:30 – Harvest is bought (60 s)

_Co-op staff → Collections → Record purchase._

- Find Aminata → rice, 1,000 kg → the co-op's price fills in → **Le 4,000**.
  _Switch to co-op manager → open the batch → Approve._

> "Someone else has to approve it. The person who weighs never approves their own purchase."

- Show the payment: **Smart Split**, with the farmer's share paid straight to her Orange Money, **15% saved in her Harvest Lock**, and co-op dues taken out.

> "AgriLink never holds the money. Each share goes directly from the co-op's account to where it belongs."

- Show the SMS receipt Aminata got.

### 1:30 – Aminata's phone (60 s)

_Aminata → Savings._

- Harvest Lock balance, **"unlocks 1 April for planting"**.
  _My passport._
- Score out of 100 built from **her real sales and savings**, not from collateral.
- Tips: "Keep 3 records this season to add 5 points."

> "Her gender, age and where she lives are never part of the score. And no lender sees it unless she says yes with her PIN."

### 2:30 – She gets fertiliser on credit (75 s)

_Loans → Rice & groundnut input loan → "You can borrow up to Le 1,000"._

- Enter Le 800 → **Read the terms**: amount, fee, total, "up to 50% of each sale until repaid, you always keep 30%, no extra charges if late".
- Enter PIN → applied.
  _Switch to Rokel Community Bank → Applications → Approve._
- A voucher code is sent to Aminata by SMS.

> "She doesn't get cash. She gets a voucher that only works for seed, fertiliser and crop protection at approved dealers."

### 3:45 – At the agro-dealer (45 s)

_Makeni Agro Inputs → Redeem voucher._

- Type the code → **"Aminata S." · Le 800 left** → add 2 bags of NPK → **Send code to farmer**.
- Aminata's phone gets: _"Makeni Agro wants Le 700 for NPK ×2. Give this code only if you agree."_ → enter it → done.

> "The dealer is paid by the bank the same day. Aminata only owes what she actually took."

### 4:30 – The loop closes (30 s)

_Co-op records Aminata's next sale → approve → open the payment._

- **Loan repayment comes out first, then savings, then her share.** Her loan is repaid, her score goes up and her limit grows to Le 1,500.

> "One harvest sale paid her, saved for her and repaid her loan, with no cash and no extra trips. That's AgriLink."

### Close (15 s)

> "It runs on the mobile money Sierra Leone already has. It's built, tested and ready for a pilot with one co-op, one community bank and one agro-dealer in Bombali."

## Questions to be ready for

| Question                                  | Short answer                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Isn't this just mobile money?"           | No. We use mobile money as the rail. We add harvest-linked splitting, savings, records and a credit passport, which MMOs don't offer.                              |
| "Who holds the money?"                    | Licensed partners only: MMOs, the bank or the custodian. AgriLink sends instructions and keeps the records.                                                        |
| "What about farmers without smartphones?" | SMS today for receipts, vouchers and confirmations. USSD menus are next on the roadmap. Agents and co-ops can assist, but the farmer's own PIN is always required. |
| "How do you make money?"                  | See application-checklist.md: business model.                                                                                                                      |
| "What if the farmer can't repay?"         | Repayment is capped per sale, the farmer always keeps 30%, there are no late fees, and the first loans are small and grow only after repaying on time.             |
| "Is it live?"                             | It's an MVP with demo data and mock payments. A pilot with real partners is the next step.                                                                         |
